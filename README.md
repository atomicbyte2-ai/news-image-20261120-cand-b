"ONE DAY IN UTC" — a cream broadsheet front page summing up Friday 20 November 2026 in 50 stories, all first reported that day. A giant serif date masthead, a time-ordered "THE DAY IN UTC" timeline strip (overnight → late), a day-in-numbers strip (300,000 evacuated · 9–6 ICJ vote · $1.8bn GTA VI · 25.9bn km Voyager 1 · Dow −1,100), four illustrated lead stories (Shenzhen trilateral declaration, Crimean Bridge hit, Houthi missile on Riyadh's KAFD, Cyclone Akvan landfall), twelve illustrated secondary stories, a numbered-pin world map, the ALSO ON list (17–50), and a DATE CHECK box listing the 19 Nov stories deliberately left out. Every headline and number is exact programmatic text; all illustrations are flat vector-style drawings made with Python PIL.

MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
